# Code of conduct

OpenAtelier follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and take criticism of
work gracefully, and focus on what's best for the project and the people in it.
Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing others' private
information are not acceptable, in any project space — issues, pull requests,
discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If something happens that isn't OK, report it privately to the maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainers will
respond as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe, from a warning
up to a ban from the project.
